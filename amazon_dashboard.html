What the Data Revealed

Key Insights

Set is the Top Revenue Category

With ₹357M in revenue and 43,033 units sold, Set dominated both quantity and revenue outperforming Kurta despite a smaller price gap than expected.

Maharashtra Leads by State

Maharashtra generated the highest revenue at ₹122M, followed by Karnataka and Telangana. The top 3 states alone account for over 27% of total revenue.

April was the Peak Month

Revenue peaked in April then declined through June, suggesting potential seasonality or demand patterns worth monitoring in future campaigns.

14.2% Cancellation Rate

Over 18,000 orders were cancelled out of 128,941 total making it one of the most significant patterns visible in the order status breakdown.

60%+ Orders Still In Transit

The majority of orders were still in transit, indicating data was captured mid-fulfillment cycle important context for interpreting delivered vs. shipped figures.

Merchant vs Amazon Fulfillment

A significant portion fulfilled by Merchants rather than Amazon directly a factor that may correlate with higher cancellation and return rates.

How It Was Built

Analysis Process

1

Data Collection

Sourced Amazon India sales dataset with 128K+ rows covering order status, categories, states, and revenue data.

2

Data Cleaning

Cleaned in Excel handled null values, standardized state names, fixed inconsistent status codes, and created a Status Group column.

3

EDA in Excel

Built pivot tables to explore revenue by month, state, and category. Identified key KPIs including AOV and cancellation rate.

4

Dashboard in Power BI

Imported clean data to Power BI, created DAX measures, and built an interactive dashboard with slicers for dynamic filtering.
