Data Source

The Dataset

This project uses the Olist Brazilian E-Commerce public dataset from Kaggle — one of the most comprehensive real-world e-commerce datasets available. It consists of 9 relational tables covering the full order lifecycle from purchase to review.

All tables were ingested into PostgreSQL as-is, then cleaned and transformed through an ETL pipeline before being loaded into Power BI for visualization.

What the Data Revealed

Key Insights

Health Beauty is the Top Category

Health Beauty led all categories with over R$ 1.8M in revenue, followed closely by Watches Gifts and Bed Bath Table — suggesting strong demand in personal care and home goods.

São Paulo Dominates by State

SP generated R$ 5.9M — nearly 3x the second-highest state. The top 3 states (SP, RJ, MG) account for a significant share of total platform revenue.

Revenue Peaked in May 2018

Monthly revenue climbed steadily through early 2018, peaking in May before a sharp decline through September — a potential seasonality signal worth monitoring.

Majority of Reviews are 5-Star

The review score distribution shows that most customers gave 5-star ratings, indicating high overall satisfaction with the platform and its sellers.

97% Orders Delivered

Nearly all orders reached "delivered" status with a 91.89% on-time rate, demonstrating strong logistics reliability across the platform.

Cancellation Rate Only 0.63%

With a very low cancellation rate of 0.63%, the platform shows healthy order completion — a stark contrast to many other e-commerce markets.

How It Was Built

Analysis Process

1

Data Ingestion

Loaded 9 CSV tables into PostgreSQL as-is using pgAdmin for raw data management.

2

SQL ETL

Cleaned and transformed data with SQL — null handling, standardization, deduplication, and calculated columns.

3

SQL Analysis

Ran analysis queries covering revenue trends, RFM segmentation, delivery performance, and seller rankings.

4

Power BI Dashboard

Imported clean tables to Power BI, built DAX measures, and designed an interactive dashboard with dynamic slicers.

5

Insight Extraction

Synthesized findings into actionable insights on revenue, regional performance, and customer behavior.
